NITRO LANES

Privacy Policy · Developer: VirtualXone LLC · Last updated: 27 September 2026

This policy explains what information the Nitro Lanes game (Android app and web version) collects, why, and how you can delete it.

Playing as a guest

You can play without an account. Guest progress (cash, cars, upgrades, settings, best scores) is stored only on your device and is never sent to us.

What we collect if you create an account

DataWhy
UsernameTo sign you in and show your name on the global leaderboard. Usernames are visible to other players.
PasswordTo sign you in. It is stored only in hashed form by our authentication provider; we can never see it.
Game progress (cash, owned cars, tuning, cosmetics, settings)Cloud save, so your progress follows you between devices.
Scores, distance, world and car used for your best run in each modeThe global leaderboard (public).

We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, advertising ID or device identifiers. The game contains no ads, no analytics and no third-party trackers, and we never sell or share your data.

Where it is stored

Account data is stored with Supabase, our database and sign-in provider, and is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Supabase processes it only on our behalf.

App permissions

  • Internet — sign-in, cloud save and leaderboard. The game itself works offline.
  • Vibration — haptic feedback (can be turned off in Settings).

Deleting your account and data

In the app (instant):

  1. Open Nitro Lanes and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings and scroll to Delete account.
  3. Tap Delete and type your username to confirm.

This immediately and permanently deletes your account, username, cloud save and all leaderboard entries. Nothing is kept.

Without the app: email mqasimazhar@googlemail.com with the subject “Nitro Lanes account deletion” and your username. We will delete the account and all its data within 30 days and confirm by email.

Guest progress lives only on your device — uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it.

Retention

We keep account data only while your account exists. When you delete it, everything listed above is removed right away.

Children

Nitro Lanes is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
